Jellyfin desktop client · Windows · Linux · macOS

Your Jellyfin library, played by mpv.

MediaFlick is a native desktop app for Jellyfin. It plays straight away with a built-in mpv player, or hands off to your own mpv with every shader, script and profile intact. Progress and watched state sync back to the server either way.

01 · Playback

It's mpv either way.

The built-in player is libmpv inside the app window, under MediaFlick's own controls. Already tuned mpv to the last shader? Switch to external mpv and MediaFlick launches the executable you use today.

  • Plays on first launch. Windows releases and the Linux AppImage bundle libmpv. No player to install.
  • One window. Native video with MediaFlick's controls over it, including subtitle styling and a shortcut recorder.
  • Original quality by default. Or Jellyfin's automatic limit, or a fixed cap from 1.5 to 120 Mbps.
~/.config/mpv/mpv.confUnchanged
profile=gpu-hq
vo=gpu-next
glsl-shaders-append="~~/shaders/FSRCNNX_x2.glsl"
target-colorspace-hint=yes
input-conf="~~/input.conf"
script-opts-append=svp-enabled=yes
  • Everything carries over. SVP4, GLSL shaders, Lua scripts, HDR profiles and input.conf bindings apply as usual.
  • Jellyfin still keeps count. One persistent IPC connection reports start, progress, watched state and resume position.
  • Easy on Windows. Download mpv from inside the app. Linux and macOS use the system mpv they find.

02 · Library

Search that doesn't wait for the server.

MediaFlick mirrors your libraries into a local SQLite database and keeps it current in the background. Type-ahead search runs on your machine, across titles, overviews, genres and cast, so a big library stays instant.

03 · Browse

Made for looking at posters.

Home rows for Continue Watching, Next Up and everything new. Hover a card for its details and actions. Open a title for cast, seasons, episodes and exactly what's in the file. Scroll to walk through it.

  1. Step 01

    Land on Home.

    A billboard for what's new, then shelves for Continue Watching, Next Up and every library, laid out for posters rather than lists.

  2. Step 02

    Hover a card.

    Play, watchlist, rating and the synopsis appear over the shelf. Nothing to click into, nothing to back out of.

  3. Step 03

    Open a film.

    Resume where you were, browse the cast, and see exactly what's in the file: container, codecs, bitrate, audio and subtitle tracks.

  4. Step 04

    Or a series.

    The next episode up top, seasons and episodes below, and unwatched episodes kept spoiler-free until you reveal them.

04 · Playstate

Jellyfin always knows where you stopped.

Playing outside a browser usually means the server loses track. MediaFlick reports playback start, progress, watched state and resume position while you watch, so every other device picks up where you left off.

05 · Companion

Plug in your stack. Keep the keys on the server.

The optional MediaFlick Companion is a Jellyfin plugin. It brings Sonarr, Radarr, Seerr, MDBList and TMDB into the app through typed, authenticated calls. Service addresses and API keys never leave the server.
