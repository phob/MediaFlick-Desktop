Recently added

Built around your player

Keep everything you tuned in mpv.

MediaFlick launches the real mpv process. Your config, shaders, scripts, HDR profiles and input bindings all come with it.

01

External mpv playback

Play original-quality streams through your installed mpv, with optional Jellyfin transcoding when the connection needs it.

02

A local, searchable library

MediaFlick mirrors metadata into SQLite, so browsing and search stay responsive even with a large library.

03

Playback stays in sync

Start, progress, watched state and resume position report back to Jellyfin while mpv handles the video.

04

Optional server companion

Add calendar data, requests, ratings and collection tools without exposing service credentials to desktop clients.

How it works

The app browses. mpv plays.

  1. 1

    Connect Jellyfin

    Sign in with your password or Jellyfin Quick Connect.

  2. 2

    Choose something to watch

    Browse your local library mirror and pick a title.

  3. 3

    Hand off to mpv

    Your external player opens while MediaFlick handles sync.
