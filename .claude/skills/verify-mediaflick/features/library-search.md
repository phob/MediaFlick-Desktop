# Library and search

The library shows a virtualized grid of poster cards for Movies, Series or Favorites, with sort, direction and filter controls, an A–Z rail on name-sorted views, and an unwatched-episode count on series cards. Typing in the sidebar search shows matching titles in the same grid; an exact person match adds a "With <name>" section below it.

## Sub-features

- `library-kind` opens Movies, Series or Favorites from the sidebar.
- `library-sort` sorts by name, release date, date added, rating, date played or a seeded random order; Movies add play count and runtime, Series add date episode added. A toggle reverses the direction; Random has Shuffle again instead.
- `library-filter` filters by genre, release decade, watch status (including In progress), parental rating, series status (Series only) and favorites, with removable chips.
- `library-letters` jumps to a letter from the A–Z rail while the view sorts by name without a search.
- `library-unwatched` shows a series' unwatched episode count on its card.
- `library-search` searches from the sidebar after a short debounce.
- `library-empty` shows `Nothing to show` with a reason.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Sidebar `Movies` (`/library?kind=Movie`), `Series` (`/library?kind=Series`), `Favorites` (`/library?favorite=true`).
- Sidebar search field `Search the library`. Off Home, pointing at the collapsed sidebar expands it to show the field.
- A shelf's `All` link on Home, and genre badges on detail pages.

## Driving it with ctx (just verify)

Preconditions:

- Signed in to the demo server with `ctx.signIn`.

- **Movies.** `ctx.press({ role: "link", name: "Movies" })`. `ctx.pathname()` is `/library?kind=Movie`, `{ role: "heading", name: "Movies" }` and `{ role: "combobox", name: "Sort by" }` (value `Sort: Name`) appear, and cards `a[aria-label^="Open details for"]` fill the grid. The count is an `aria-live` span reading `N items` or `1 item`, with a thousands separator from 1,000 (`5,152 items`). *Proven by `browse`, `home-library`* (path, heading, `Sort by` and cards); the `Sort: Name` value on a fresh Movies view is source-only (`library-parity` reads it only on a hand-written Movies URL), and the separator was observed on the test server, not asserted.
- **Search.** `ctx.fill({ role: "textbox", name: "Search the library" }, "night of the living")`. After about 200 ms `ctx.pathname()` is `/library?search=night%20of%20the%20living` and `{ role: "heading", name: "Results for “night of the living”" }` appears. *Proven by `browse`.* Enter commits at once (source: the search form's submit flushes the debounce; not driven). A term under 2 characters never commits, and clearing the field (or shortening it below 2 characters) leaves a search at once for `/library`, the Movies view. *Clearing proven by `library-filters`*; shortening is source-only.
- **Search off Home.** Off Home the sidebar is collapsed to icons and has no search field: `ctx.hover({ css: '[data-sidebar="sidebar"]' })` expands it, then fill as above. *Proven by `browse`, `home-library`.*
- **Open a result.** Wait for the search route first, then `ctx.press({ role: "link", name: "Open details for Night of the Living Dead" })`. *Proven by `browse`, `item-detail`.*
- **No match.** Searching `zzqqxx` shows `Nothing to show` and `Nothing matches “zzqqxx”.` *Proven by `home-library`.* A filtered view with no search and no match says `No items match that query.` instead (on the demo server, Series with `Continuing`; observed in a maintenance run).
- **Series.** Off Home, hover the sidebar and press `{ role: "link", name: "Series" }`: `/library?kind=Series` with `{ role: "heading", name: "Series" }`. *Proven by `library-filters`.*
- **Sort.** `ctx.choose({ name: "Sort by" }, "Sort: Release date")`; the URL gains `sort=year` and `filters=true` as a new history entry. Options are `Sort: Name` (`name`), `Sort: Release date` (`year`), `Sort: Date added` (`added`; `Sort: Date show added` on Series), `Sort: Date episode added` (`episode-added`, Series only), `Sort: Rating` (`rating`), `Sort: Date played` (`played`), `Sort: Play count` (`plays`, Movies only), `Sort: Runtime` (`runtime`, Movies only) and `Sort: Random` (`random`). A mixed view such as Favorites offers only the shared ones. *Proven by `library-parity` (every option on Movies and Series, Favorites' list), `home-library` and `library-filters`.*
- **Direction.** Next to the sort, a button named for what it does: `Sort descending` while ascending, `Sort ascending` while descending. The URL carries `order=asc|desc` only when it differs from the sort's natural direction (Name ascending, every other sort descending); *Proven by `library-parity`.* Choosing another sort starts it in its natural direction (source; not driven from a reversed sort).
- **Random.** `Sort: Random` adds `seed=<u32>`; the same seed gives the same order after a reload, and the `Shuffle again` button (replacing the direction toggle) picks a new seed. *Proven by `library-parity`.*
- **A–Z rail.** With the Name sort and no search, `{ role: "navigation", name: "Jump to letter" }` holds buttons `Jump to #`, `Jump to A` … `Jump to Z`; letters without titles are disabled. A press scrolls the grid so that letter's first title's row is at the top, or as far as the grid can scroll. Letters come from the server's sort name (`The Boy in the Plastic Bubble` files under B). `GET /api/items/letters` takes the `/api/items` parameters and answers `{ letters: { "B": 0, … } }` in the grid's current order. *Proven by `library-parity` (Movies both directions, Series).*
- **Filters.** `{ role: "button", name: "Filters" }` (name gains `, N active`) opens a menu whose accessible name is the trigger's current name (`Filters`, or `Filters, 1 active` once a filter is on), with the visible label `Filter library`. Its sub-menu triggers are menuitems named with their current value: `Genre Any`, `Release decade Any`, `Watch status Any watch status`, `Parental rating Any`, `Series status Any` (Series view only), `Favorites Any`, then `Clear all filters`. Options are `menuitemradio`s: `Any genre` then the server's genres (`genre=`), `Any decade` then `2020s` … `1900s` (`decade=1960`), `Any watch status`, `Unwatched`, `In progress`, `Watched` (`watched=unwatched|progress|watched`), `Any rating` then the certificates in the view's kinds (`parental=PG`, from `GET /api/official-ratings?kind=Movie`), `Any status`, `Continuing`, `Ended` (`seriesStatus=`), `All titles`, `Favorites only`. A Series-only filter or a sort the view cannot apply is ignored when read from the URL. Picking an option closes the whole menu; reopen `Filters` for the next one. *Proven by `home-library` (Release decade), `library-filters` (Genre, Watch status) and `library-parity` (In progress, Parental rating, Series status, each count against the server: In progress against the server's user data under the app's definition (see Gotchas), Parental rating and Series status against Jellyfin's `OfficialRatings` and `SeriesStatus` queries).*
- **Chips.** Active filters sit in `{ role: "group", name: "Active filters" }`, each a button `Remove <label> filter` (`Remove Released: 1960s filter`, `Remove Genre: Action filter`, `Remove Unwatched filter`, `Remove In progress filter`, `Remove Rated: PG filter`, `Remove Ended filter`, `Remove Favorites filter`), followed by `Clear all`. Removing the last chip, or `Clear all`, removes the group and keeps the sort. *Proven by `home-library`, `library-filters`.*
- **Favorites.** `ctx.press({ role: "link", name: "Favorites" })` lands on `/library?favorite=true` with `{ role: "heading", name: "Favorites" }`, `Filters, 1 active` and the chip `Remove Favorites filter`. *Proven by `home-library`.*
- **Unwatched badge.** A series card with unwatched episodes has `[data-unplayed-count="N"]` in its poster's top-right corner; its text includes `N unwatched episodes`, or `1 unwatched episode` (screen-reader text) and the card's `Open details for …` link is described by it. `/api/items` carries `unplayedCount` on Series rows. *Proven by `library-parity` against the server's `UserData.UnplayedItemCount`.*
- **Proof.** `05-filtered.png`, `06-empty-search.png` and `07-favorites.png` from `home-library`, `01-series.png`, `05-two-filters.png` and `library-filters.json` from `library-filters`, `parity-report.json`, `11-movies-controls.png` and `12-series-controls.png` from `library-parity`, and the search steps in `browse`'s `steps.log`.

## Gotchas

- Movie In progress is "part-way through": a resume point and not played. Jellyfin's `IsResumable` also counts played titles that still have a resume point (rewatches), so the two differ when such titles exist (demo server, 2026-10-12: app 4, `IsResumable` 7). `library-parity` asserts the app's definition against the server's user data and records `IsResumable` without asserting it.
- `Sort: Rating` orders by Jellyfin's community rating, not by Companion (MDBList) ratings; those only add card badges, and only once rating sources are picked in Appearance (default: none).
- The A–Z rail files any title whose sort name starts with a non-ASCII letter or a digit under `#`.
- Series In progress is defined by episodes: some episode watched or started, and some present episode unwatched. `library-parity` asserts it against that definition applied to the server's episode user data, and records Jellyfin's own `IsResumable` count for Series without asserting it (both were 1 on the demo server).
- The demo server has eleven movies and one series, so the whole grid fits on screen and an A–Z jump cannot scroll a late letter to the top; the drive then checks the grid scrolled as far as it can.
- Watch state on the demo account changes while a drive runs. `library-parity` compares each watch-dependent count until the app and the server agree, for up to 90 s.

- A search commits exactly `/library?search=<term>`: it drops `kind`, the sort and every filter, so a search from Series covers movies too (observed in a maintenance run). It also covers seasons and episodes (subtitle `Series · S1E2`) and matches genre words and original titles, which a large library shows and the demo server hides; Favorites likewise includes favorited episodes and seasons (`src/library/query.rs`). Results are ordered by relevance; `Sort by` and the direction toggle still render and write the URL but do not reorder them (`order_by` in `src/library/query.rs`).
- The search commits with `navigate(..., { replace: true })` 200 ms after typing stops. Pressing a card before that is overwritten by the search navigation; wait for the exact `/library?search=` route.
- The grid is virtualized: only visible rows exist in the DOM. Search for a title instead of scrolling for it.
- `Night of the Living Dead` also appears in the Movies grid; a press before the search commits can open it from the wrong page.
- The sidebar stays expanded over the grid while the pointer rests on it, which it does after `ctx.fill` into the search field; focus alone does not hold it open. `ctx.press` waits, then parks the pointer if a card is still covered.
- The collapsed sidebar's `Search` icon is a plain link to `/library` (the Movies view), and an invisible group label covers it. Pointing at the sidebar expands it into the search field first, so the field is the pointer path.
- People never appear in the grid. An exact Jellyfin person match adds a `Titles with <name>` section (headed `With <name>`) below it. Separately, any search adds a `Not in your library` section of Seerr results when `/api/seerr/status` reports `linked`; it stays hidden when Seerr has nothing new or fails (`ui/src/components/seerr/NotInYourLibrary.tsx`). Source-only; the demo server has no Companion.
- A search needs a letter or digit to match anything: `!!` commits (it has two characters) and shows `Nothing to show`, with `/api/items` answering 200 and no items, while `night!!` searches for `night`. *Proven by `punctuation-search`.*
- The library defaults to `kind=Movie` when no `kind`, `search` or `favorite` is given. Plain Movies and Series views can also apply remembered filters or "hide watched" without showing them in the URL; both preferences are off in a fresh profile.
