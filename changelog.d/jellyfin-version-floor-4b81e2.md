### Added

- MediaFlick Desktop now requires Jellyfin 10.11.0 or newer. On an older server the sign-in screen names the server's version and the minimum, and sign-in and Quick Connect are refused before any credentials are sent.
- On a Jellyfin server older than 12.0.0, **Settings → MediaFlick Companion** now says that the Companion needs Jellyfin 12.0.0 or newer, instead of saying it was not found.
