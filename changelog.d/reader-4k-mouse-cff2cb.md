### Changed

- Reflowable books (EPUB, MOBI, AZW, AZW3) size their text and column to the reader window, so a book on a 4K screen reads as large as on a 1080p one, also from the sofa. The text size setting still scales it from there.

### Added

- Page through any book with the mouse: left click on the page goes forward, right click goes back. The right-click menu now shows only outside the page, such as on the reader's footer.
