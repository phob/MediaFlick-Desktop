### Changed

- The Home Upcoming shelf is now the Release Timeline. It uses the same poster cards as other shelves and marks today between released and upcoming titles. With the Companion, about a third of the shelf shows releases from the past 30 days, each marked as downloaded or missing, so an episode or disc release that should have arrived but hasn't stands out.
- Movies not yet in your library show their TMDB poster on the Release Timeline (requires an updated Companion with TMDB configured).
