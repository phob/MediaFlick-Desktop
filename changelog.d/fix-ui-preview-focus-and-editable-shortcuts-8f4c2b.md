### Fixed

- The Appearance settings live preview now renders non-interactive preview cards directly instead of intercepting links and inline buttons after render, keeping hover previews working without risking accidental navigation or library actions.
- Opening the book reader's Go to or Appearance panels now moves keyboard focus to the slider as soon as it becomes visible instead of polling across animation frames.
- Select-all shortcuts now work reliably inside nested editable text regions as well as top-level editable fields.
