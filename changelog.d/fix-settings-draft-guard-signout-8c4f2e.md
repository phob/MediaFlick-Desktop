### Fixed

- Signing out from the sidebar menu or deleting local account data while a Settings shelf has unsaved changes now asks before discarding drafts instead of silently dropping them; choosing **Keep editing** cancels the action and preserves your edits, while confirming discards them cleanly and proceeds.
- Unsaved edits on device-level Settings shelves (Player, Playback, and Application) now survive account status changes such as session expiry instead of being reset.
