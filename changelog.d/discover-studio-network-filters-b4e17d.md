### Added

- Discover can filter popular movies by studio, with a type-ahead studio search, and popular series by network, from a list of major streaming services and broadcasters. Both combine with the genre, decade, sort and score filters, and the active studio or network shows as a chip that clears it. Studios on a movie's Discover page and networks on a series' Discover page open Discover with that filter applied. This needs an updated MediaFlick Companion; with an older one the controls stay hidden.
