### Added

- Library and Discover detail pages show a Crew rail under the cast with headshots of the creators, directors, writers, producers, and composers (and, on Discover, the director of photography). Someone credited for several of these jobs appears once with all of them. Selecting a face opens that person's titles.

### Changed

- A person's page now lists titles they worked on as crew, not only acting credits, both on your server and in Discover.
- Guest stars appear in the Cast rail.
- The crew lines in a title's Details panel, and Discover's "Created by", "Directed by", and "Written by" lines, are replaced by the Crew rail. Discover shows no Crew rail until the Companion is updated.
