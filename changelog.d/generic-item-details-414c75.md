### Added

- Every Jellyfin item now has a details page. Albums, tracks, box sets, books, photos, folders and other items that are not movies or shows get a simple page with their artwork, year, runtime, overview, genres, credits and studios, and folders and albums list their contents. These pages have no playback, favorites or ratings yet.

### Changed

- Square artwork such as album covers is shown whole on cards instead of being cropped to a poster.

### Fixed

- A watched or favorite change that the live connection missed, for example while reconnecting, now refreshes Home, title pages and open grids when the next library check picks it up.
