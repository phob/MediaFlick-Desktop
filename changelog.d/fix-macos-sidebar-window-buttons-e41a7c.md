### Fixed

- macOS: the window now has its close, minimize and zoom buttons. Close quits MediaFlick in the same orderly way as Cmd+Q, or minimizes the window when Close behavior is set to Minimize window. The green button takes the window full screen and back, and the window reopens at the size and position it was left at.
- The side menu off Home no longer collapses and re-expands with every pointer move when MediaFlick is used over macOS Screen Sharing. It now collapses a quarter of a second after the pointer leaves it.
