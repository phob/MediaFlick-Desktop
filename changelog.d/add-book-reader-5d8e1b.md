### Added

- Books open in a built-in reader: EPUB, MOBI, AZW, AZW3, PDF and comic archives (CBZ, CBR including RAR 5, CB7, CBT). A book's page offers Read, Continue at the place you left, or Read again once finished. The reader fills the window and works entirely from the keyboard or a TV remote: arrows and Page Up/Down turn pages, the next/previous track keys move between chapters, Enter or the Menu key opens the menu, and Back closes the top panel and then the book.
- The exact place in each book is kept per account, and Jellyfin gets the position in the units jellyfin-web uses, so other clients show it too. Reaching the end marks the book played. Scripts in books never run, and the Jellyfin token never reaches the reader.
