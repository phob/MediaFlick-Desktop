### Added

- Settings → Viewing → Startup destination lists every library under its Jellyfin name, so the app can open on any library.

### Changed

- An earlier Movies or Series startup choice now opens your first Movies or TV shows library, or Home if you have none. The choice is kept as it was until you save Viewing settings. If the app has not read your library list yet, for example right after you sign in, it opens Home that one time.
- If your startup library is removed from the server, the app opens Home instead.
